# Plancy Asia — Company Overview

## 🚀 About Plancy Asia

**Plancy Asia** is a modern software development and digital solutions company focused on delivering **cloud-based platforms, ERP systems, and scalable business applications** across ASEAN markets.

Founded in **2021**, Plancy Asia has rapidly evolved into a **growth-driven tech company**, providing end-to-end solutions including:

- Web & Mobile Application Development  
- Cloud Hosting & Server Infrastructure  
- ERP & Business Automation Systems  
- E-commerce & Sales Platforms  
- Digital Marketing & AI Automation
